About us

We are an export partner, not a factory. That distinction matters, so it is stated plainly rather than hidden in a stock photograph.

What we do

We supply polyethylene and polypropylene masterbatch to film producers, pipe extruders and injection moulders in Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa, and we handle the parts of the transaction that a domestic Chinese factory is usually bad at:

  • English technical documentation — TDS, batch COA and MSDS issued in the format your customs broker expects
  • Grade selection against your process rather than a fixed catalogue
  • Export documentation, HS classification and FOB booking from Qingdao or Shanghai
  • Trial quantities from one tonne, so a mistake costs a pallet rather than a container

What we do not do

  • We do not own a production line, and we do not claim to
  • We do not publish parameters we have not received from the producing factory
  • We do not supply recycled or reprocessed material in grades sold as virgin
  • We do not accept an order we cannot ship with a matching batch COA

How we work

1. You send the specification. Resin, process, target addition rate and destination port. If you only have a finished-product requirement, that is enough to start.

2. We match a grade and confirm the datasheet. You receive the TDS before any payment is discussed, so you can check the numbers against your own requirements.

3. We ship a trial quantity. One tonne, or a 25 kg sample bag with its batch COA, so you can run it on your own line.

4. Repeat orders move to deposit terms. Once the trial passes, pricing and terms improve with volume.

Partner factories

Production runs at partner plants in Shandong and Guangdong, the two clusters where filler, black and white masterbatch capacity is concentrated in China. We work with producers that hold ISO 9001 and can issue batch-level COAs, and we verify the material before it leaves the factory.

Factory certifications, production photographs and third-party test reports are supplied on request with any quotation.
