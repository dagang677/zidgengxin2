What we do differently

Most masterbatch websites describe quality. We publish the numbers behind it — and the documentation your customs broker will ask for.

Numeric specifications

Filler or pigment content, carrier resin, MFI at a stated test condition, mesh size and moisture limit. If a parameter is not measured, we say so instead of writing "adjustable".

Documentation ready for customs

English TDS, batch-specific COA and MSDS, plus HS code guidance. The COA is issued per production batch, not per product family.

Small first orders

One-ton MOQ and 25 kg sample bags let you run a trial on your own extruder before committing to a container.

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

One metric ton per grade. Mixed grades can be combined into a single pallet for a trial shipment.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes. We ship 25 kg sample bags; you cover the courier cost. A batch COA is included with the sample so you can verify the same numbers you were quoted.

Which documents come with a shipment?

English TDS, batch COA, MSDS, packing list and commercial invoice. Certificates from the producing factory (SGS, RoHS, ISO) are supplied where applicable.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

T/T in advance for trial orders, 30% deposit against a 70% balance for repeat orders. FOB Qingdao or Shanghai; CIF quotes on request.
